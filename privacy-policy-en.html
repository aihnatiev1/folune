Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Folune is an app for reading ebooks and listening to audiobooks. It is built so that your library belongs only to you.

1. Data we collect

None. Folune has no accounts, ads, analytics, trackers or third-party data-collection SDKs. We never receive your books, notes, progress or device information.

2. What stays on your device

  • Books and audiobooks you add
  • Reading and listening progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes and collections
  • App settings
  • Technical logs and crash reports (MetricKit), which leave the device only if you tap “Export Diagnostics” and send the file yourself

3. iCloud sync (Folune Plus)

If you turn on sync, your library, progress, bookmarks, notes and covers are stored in your private iCloud database, and — if you choose — book files in your iCloud Drive. Only your Apple Account can access this data; the developer cannot. Apple processes it under the Apple Privacy Policy. Turning sync off never deletes data from your device; you can delete the iCloud copy in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

4. Network

  • The Catalog contacts only the sources you open (Wikisource, Project Gutenberg, or OPDS catalogs you add) to list and download books. Nothing about your library is sent to them.
  • From Computer runs a temporary server on your local Wi-Fi network only while that screen is open; files go straight to your device.

5. Purchases

Folune Plus subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details; the app only receives Apple’s confirmation that a purchase is active.

6. Children

Folune is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, children included.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
